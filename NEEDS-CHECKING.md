# Before launch: things to confirm by phone

Researched 2026-10-07. Main source: NYS DOH syringe program directory dated 2026-07-23
(health.ny.gov `sep_hours_sites.pdf`); the city's own SSP guide is from 2022 and stale.

## Conflicting info
- **BOOM!Health**: 718-295-2666 (its site) vs 718-292-7718 (state directory). Site number is listed.
- **AIDS Center of Queens County**: site and state directory disagree on addresses, so address is blank.
- **Positive Health Project** (now under Housing Works, 301 W 37th St): floor and hours differ by source.
- **OnPoint NYC**: hours differ between city, state and OnPoint's own site, so no hours are listed.
- **Ali Forney Center**: calls its drop-in 24/7 but lists limited hours.

## Couldn't confirm on the org's own site (marked `medium`)
- Bronx Móvil and Family Services Network of NY (sites failed TLS; both are in the state directory).
- Bellevue Street Health Outreach (no program page; state directory only).
- The 5 DHS drop-in centers and shelter intake (undated city flyer, no hours or phones).
- OASAS treatment finder, N-CAP naloxone co-pay, HepCAP (confirmed only via city pages).
- NEXT Distro (New York coverage unclear). Covenant House NY (main site rate-limited).

## Left out on purpose
- **SWOP Brooklyn**: swopbrooklyn.org now redirects to an unrelated site. Possibly hijacked.
- **Crown Heights and Riverside sexual health clinics**: "closed until further notice."
- **Washington Heights CORNER Project / NYHRE**: merged into OnPoint NYC (2022).
- **NYC Well**: now NYC 988; listed under that name.
- **NYC DOHMH Rapid Response** syringe team: "please call," may not serve the public.
